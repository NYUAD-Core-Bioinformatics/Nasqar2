## DESeq2 Interactive Application

This tutorial walks you through using our interactive DESeq2 application for differential gene expression analysis. Our web-based interface eliminates the need for coding or command-line operations—simply upload your data, configure your analysis through intuitive menus, and explore your results through interactive visualizations.

The application is built around [DESeq2](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/html/DESeq2.html), bringing powerful statistical methods for RNA-seq analysis to your browser with a user-friendly point-and-click workflow.

### What You Can Do

Through this interactive application, you can:

- **Upload and validate** your RNA-seq count matrices with instant feedback on data quality
- **Design your experiment** using simple dropdown menus and checkboxes, supporting both simple and complex multi-factorial designs
- **Explore your data** before analysis with built-in quality control and normalization preview
- **Detect hidden batch effects** using our integrated Surrogate Variable Analysis (SVA) tool
- **Run differential expression analysis** with a single click after configuring your parameters
- **Generate publication-ready visualizations** including PCA plots, heatmaps, volcano plots, and Venn diagrams
- **Download results** in various formats for further analysis or publication

No R programming knowledge required—everything happens through interactive controls and instant visual feedback.

For background on the statistical methods used, see the [DESeq2 documentation](https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/vignettes/DESeq2/inst/doc/DESeq2.html) and [SVA workflow](https://www.bioconductor.org/packages/devel/workflows/vignettes/rnaseqGene/inst/doc/rnaseqGene.html#using-sva-with-deseq2)



### Uploading Your Data

To begin, you'll upload your RNA-seq count data through our file upload interface. The application accepts CSV files containing raw gene count matrices, which can be generated by GeneCountMerger or exported from other upstream tools.

**How to upload:**

1. Click the upload button or drag-and-drop your CSV file
2. If your data includes both Ensembl IDs and gene symbols, select the "Gene IDs and Gene Symbol" option from the dropdown menu
3. The application will automatically validate your file format and display a preview

You can start fresh with new data or continue from a previous GeneCountMerger session—the interface adapts to your workflow.

#### Reviewing Your Data

Once your file uploads successfully, the application displays your count matrix in an interactive table where you can:

- **Browse through your data** using pagination controls
- **Sort columns** by clicking on column headers
- **Search for specific genes** using the built-in search box
- **Identify issues** automatically—the app highlights any duplicate gene names in red and displays them in a separate panel for easy review

Duplicate gene names are automatically handled by appending unique IDs, ensuring your analysis runs smoothly without manual data cleaning.

### Setting Up Your Analysis

#### Configuring Your Experimental Design

Before running the analysis, you'll configure your experimental design through an interactive form:

1. **Select your factors** from dropdown menus populated with your sample metadata
2. **Build your design formula** by clicking on condition variables—the app constructs the formula for you
3. **Preview your design** to ensure it matches your experimental setup
4. **Click "Initialize DESeq2"** to prepare the analysis with your chosen parameters

The interface guides you through design formula creation with tooltips and examples, making it accessible even if you're unfamiliar with statistical modeling syntax. Click the help icon for detailed information about design formulas and best practices.

#### Running Surrogate Variable Analysis (Optional)

After initializing your DESeq2 parameters, the application offers an optional batch effect detection step:

1. **Enable SVA** by checking the "Run Surrogate Variable Analysis" box
2. **Set the number of surrogate variables** using the slider or input field (or let the app auto-detect)
3. **Click "Run SVA"** and watch the progress bar as the analysis executes
4. **Review the results** in automatically generated diagnostic plots showing detected batch effects

If surrogate variables are detected, you can choose to include them as adjustment factors in your differential expression analysis—just check the box next to each surrogate variable you want to include. The interface makes it easy to see the impact of batch correction on your data.

### Exploring Your Results

Once the analysis completes, the application opens several interactive tabs where you can explore your results. Each tab is designed for specific aspects of your analysis:

#### Interactive Tabs

Navigate between these tabs to explore different aspects of your results:

- **VST Tab**: View variance-stabilized transformed data with interactive PCA plots—hover over points to see sample labels, click to highlight groups
- **DE Results Tab**: Browse the differential expression results in a sortable, filterable table—click column headers to sort, use the search box to find specific genes
- **Gene BoxPlot Tab**: Search for any gene and instantly see its expression across samples in an interactive boxplot with individual data points
- **Heatmap Tab**: Generate customizable heatmaps of top differentially expressed genes—adjust the number of genes, clustering method, and color scheme using the control panel
- **Volcano Plot Tab** *(appears after post-analysis)*: Interact with volcano plots showing fold-change vs significance—hover over points to see gene names, click to select genes of interest
- **Venn Diagram Tab** *(appears after comparing multiple contrasts)*: Visualize overlaps between gene sets from different comparisons with interactive, clickable regions

#### Interactive Features

Each visualization includes:
- **Zoom and pan controls** for detailed exploration
- **Hover tooltips** showing detailed information
- **Download buttons** to save high-resolution images for publications
- **Customization panels** to adjust colors, thresholds, and display options
- **Gene selection tools** to mark and export genes of interest
