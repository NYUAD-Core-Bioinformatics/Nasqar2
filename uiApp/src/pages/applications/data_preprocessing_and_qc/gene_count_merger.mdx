## Gene Count Merger

Gene Count Merger combines individual gene-count files or multiple count
matrices into one analysis-ready matrix. It supports raw counts from tools such
as HTSeq and featureCounts, and gene-level estimated counts imported from
Kallisto.

The merged bulk RNA-seq matrix can be downloaded or continued directly to
DESeq2Shiny or START. Single-cell matrices should be loaded directly into
SeuratV5Shiny in their native 10x, H5, or matrix format; Gene Count Merger does
not offer a single-cell merge route.

**Input requirement:** the first column of every matrix must contain the same
gene identifiers. When merging matrices, matching identifiers are joined even
if their row order differs.
