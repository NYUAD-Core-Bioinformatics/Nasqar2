### Choosing an analysis

| Method | Gene set used | Main results |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **GSEA** | All tested genes, ranked by a selected statistic | Enriched pathways with enrichment scores and normalized enrichment scores |
| **ORA** | Genes passing selected significance and fold-change thresholds | Over-represented GO terms and KEGG pathways |

Both applications provide searchable result tables and publication-ready plots. KEGG analysis converts the selected identifier type to NCBI Gene IDs. ORA also supports direct online KEGG pathway viewing and downloadable local Pathview PNG/PDF output.

For complete method details, see the [clusterProfiler documentation](https://yulab-smu.top/biomedical-knowledge-mining-book/).
