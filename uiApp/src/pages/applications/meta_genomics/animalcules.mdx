## Animalcules

Animalcules is an interactive application for microbiome analysis. It brings
together data summaries, diversity and differential abundance analysis,
visualisation and biomarker discovery in one interface.

For more information, see the [Animalcules documentation](https://compbiomed.github.io/animalcules-docs/).

<LaunchButton path="/animalcules/">Launch Animalcules</LaunchButton>

## Where it fits

Animalcules is the second step of the metagenomics workflow, after
[DADA2](/applications/meta_genomics/dada2).
