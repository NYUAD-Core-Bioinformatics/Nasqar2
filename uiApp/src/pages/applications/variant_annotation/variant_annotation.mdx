## Variant Annotation

The Variant Annotation application filters and annotates the variants of a VCF
file with the Bioconductor package
[VariantAnnotation](https://bioconductor.org/packages/VariantAnnotation/). It
places each variant on the transcripts it overlaps, predicts the consequence
and amino-acid change of coding variants, names the genes involved and, where
available, adds dbSNP rsIDs.

<LaunchButton path="/VariantAnnotationShiny/">Launch Variant Annotation</LaunchButton>

## What you need

- A VCF file: `.vcf`, `.vcf.gz` or `.vcf.bgz`, optionally with its `.tbi`
  index.
- **One VCF at a time, sorted by chromosome and position.**
- **Human hg38 and hg19 are the reference genomes that come with NASQAR2.**

An example VCF (a chr22 subset of the 1000 Genomes project) is included to try
the steps first.

## What you get

- A summary of the file, and filters on quality, depth, variant type,
  chromosome, region and gene.
- Annotation tables, one row per variant or one row per variant and
  transcript.
- Summary figures, downloadable as PNG, PDF and SVG.
- **Genotypes are read, and per-sample counts plotted, only for files with at
  most 50 samples.**

Large files are processed in pieces, so a whole-genome VCF can be annotated;
restricting chromosomes, regions or genes makes it faster.

## Where it fits

The genes carrying variants can be sent directly to
[ORA](/applications/rna_seq/bulk_rna/enrichment) for GO and KEGG enrichment.

## On a private instance

On a private instance you can also choose **VCF in the private space** to
read a VCF from your own storage instead of uploading it. Every analysis is
saved under a project name, and **Reopen a saved project** brings back the
filters and annotation results without recomputing. **Other species can be
added on a private instance only**: mouse, rat, zebrafish, fruit fly,
nematode, yeast, chicken, pig, cow, dog, rhesus macaque and chimpanzee. The
public site has no private space and no saved projects.
See [Run as a private instance](/quickstart/installation#run-as-a-private-instance-with-your-own-data) to set one up.
